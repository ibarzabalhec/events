ScopeEvents in Greater Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth and statewide Texas, and national events Texas builders attend, from 1 October 2026 to 31 December 2027. The organizations that hold them: builders associations, industry councils, chambers, university real estate centers and event publishers.
How events were gatheredEach event and organization was read on its own page or on the host's press release, on 29 September 2026. No aggregators and no Wikipedia. A date appears only where the organizer published it. Where the next edition has no date yet, the event shows the last edition's date and its source, and it is not drawn on the timeline.
How firms are tiedA firm is tied to an event or an organization only where that page names the firm or a person at the firm: as sponsor, exhibitor, speaker, award winner or finalist, host, board or committee member, or officer. Each tie carries the words on the page and its link. Names were matched to the Rolodex by hand. Where a page names a related company, such as Hillwood, A Perot Company, the tie keeps that name and does not point to a Rolodex card.
What could not be readHouston Business Journal and Dallas Business Journal event pages refused the reader, so their awards and events are not listed. Some calendars load only by script and showed no events: CREW Houston, CREW Dallas, CREW Fort Worth and TEXO. The Real Estate Council of Greater Fort Worth's board and sponsor lists, the IBS 2027 speaker list and the Texas Housing Conference sponsor lists did not load as text. ULI's meeting sites and Builder magazine block scripted reads. ULI dates were read with a web reader, and Builder 100 names were taken from the summit's own site.
Days to each eventCounted in the browser from the reader's date each time the page loads. An event moves to Past the day after it ends.
SourcesEach event and organization lists its pages, with the date each was read. Firm names link to their Rolodex cards.
